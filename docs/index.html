Share your whole schedule as one calendar

Keep your calendars organized by topic. Give someone one calendar they can toggle on and off that contains everything you choose to share.

How it works

  1. In the Chrome extension, tick the calendars to include. For each one, choose full details or just "busy".
  2. Calendar Grouper creates one new calendar in your Google account and keeps it in sync. New, edited and deleted events show up within a couple of minutes.
  3. Share that one calendar. The other person adds it like any Google Calendar and needs nothing installed.
Your calendars stay as they are. Nothing is merged or changed. Calendar Grouper only reads the calendars you select, and only writes to the shared calendar it creates.

Status

A personal project, built with a Chrome extension (Manifest V3), a Node.js backend on Google Cloud Run, Firestore and the Google Calendar API. Source code is on GitHub.

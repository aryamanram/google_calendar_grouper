Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Calendar Grouper is a personal project by Aryaman Ramchandran. It combines Google Calendars you choose into one shared calendar. This policy explains what it accesses, what it stores, and how to remove it.

What Calendar Grouper accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant two permissions:

  • Google Calendar (calendar): used to list your calendars, read events from the calendars you select, create the one shared calendar, copy events into it and keep them in sync, and manage who that shared calendar is shared with. Calendar Grouper never changes or deletes events on your own calendars.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email): used to identify your account and to check that it's allowed to sign in.

What is stored

Calendar Grouper keeps a small amount of data in a Google Cloud Firestore database run by the developer:

DataWhy
Google sign-in tokensTo sync your calendars while you're away
Your Google account IDTo link your settings to your account
Shared calendar settings: its name, the IDs and names of the calendars you selected, each calendar's detail level, and the email addresses you share it withTo remember your choices
Event ID pairs (an original event's ID and its copy's ID) with a last-modified timestampTo update and remove copies correctly, and to skip events that haven't changed
Sync status: progress markers, the last sync time and any error messageTo sync only what changed, and to show sync status
Event contents aren't stored by Calendar Grouper. Titles, descriptions and locations are read from your selected calendars and written straight into the shared calendar, which lives in your own Google account. They never go into Calendar Grouper's database. For calendars set to "busy", only the times are copied.

Server logs record calendar names, event counts and errors, and are kept under Google Cloud's default log retention. They don't contain event contents.

Sharing

The shared calendar's events are visible to the people you choose to share it with, through Google Calendar's normal sharing. Calendar Grouper doesn't sell, rent or share your data with anyone. It shows no ads and includes no analytics or tracking. It runs on Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Firestore, Secret Manager), which processes this data on the developer's behalf.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar Grouper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features described here, isn't used for advertising, and isn't read by people except when needed for security, to comply with the law, or with your permission.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete the shared calendar from the extension. This removes the calendar from Google and deletes its settings and event ID pairs from Calendar Grouper's database.
  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Calendar Grouper then can no longer reach your calendars.
  • Remove everything, including your stored sign-in tokens, by emailing the address below. It's deleted within 30 days.

Children

Calendar Grouper isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.
